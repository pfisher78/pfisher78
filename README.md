## Projects

**[Stocking Westminster](https://github.com/pfisher78/stocking-westminster)**: which industries pay UK MPs? A data pipeline that joins the MPs' financial interests register to Companies House, tracing declared money to official industry codes. Only about a sixth of register entries carry a company number, which is a finding in itself.


**[Bothy Map](https://github.com/pfisher78/Bothy-map)**: an offline-capable map of 107 UK mountain shelters, for places with no phone signal. [Try it live](https://pfisher78.github.io/Bothy-map/).


**[Dealership chatbot](https://github.com/pfisher78/chatbot-for-dealership)**: a Claude-powered sales and booking assistant for UK car dealers. It can only quote finance terms the dealer has signed off, because making up a monthly payment would be a regulatory problem. I shelved it before selling it.
